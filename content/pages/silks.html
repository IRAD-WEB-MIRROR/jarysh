THE NAME RULES

One or two words (two make one name with a hyphen: Kök-Jal), 18 letters max, letters only. No name of a horse foaled in the last 25 years, no famous names, no people, firms or beers. Sorry, no Aigyr.

FOR REAL?

The Owners' Club card is a Jarysh fan card. It is not a registration: real racing colours are registered with the National Equestrian Sports Directorate, and a horse's name comes from its studbook certificate. Nothing you type here leaves your browser.
