TIPPING LEAGUE RULES

1. Free. The Jarysh Tipping League is free. There is no entry fee, no deposit and no sign-up. Jarysh Points (JP) are play points: they have no cash value, cannot be bought, sold, swapped or turned into anything, and are not a wager. Jarysh does not accept bets, and this is not one.

2. What you tip. Every game of the National Ulak Tartysh League 2026 with Jarysh Odds published, in both conferences: HOME, DRAW or AWAY. Odds go up about two days before the round. In the December play-off at the State Hippodrome you pick the winner (a level game goes to a golden goal).

3. Deadline. You can tip or change a tip until the game kicks off, Irad Time. Highland kick-offs count as printed, even if the match commissioner is still on the road.

4. Points. A right tip scores the Jarysh Odds price at the time the round was published, times 10, rounded (2.35 = 24 JP). A wrong tip scores nothing. Play-off: 20 JP for the right winner.

5. Banker. Once you have tipped a game you can make it your banker for that round. A winning banker scores double. A losing banker scores nothing, same as any wrong tip. One banker per round.

6. Results. Scored against the Directorate's official match sheets, as on our scores page. A result NOT RECEIVED scores when the sheet arrives. Abandoned games score nothing.

7. The table. The weekly table puts you against Nurik and the regulars of our Tulpar channel Jarysh VIP Tips for the last round you tipped. Your season table adds up every round you played. Level on points: more right tips goes higher.

8. Your browser, your tips. Tips are kept in your own browser and are never sent to Jarysh. A private window, another phone or clearing your browser starts you from zero. We can't get them back. Honestly, we can't.

9. Sharing. After a round you can copy a line with your week and post it on Tulpar. Keep it clean-ish.

10. The Boss. Nurik always tips the favourite and always bankers his own Nurik Boost game. If you beat the Boss, tell him. He will say it was luck.

Betting in the Irad Special Economic Zone is legal only at the National Equestrian Sports Directorate's tote windows and with the state lottery IradLoto, under the Gaming and Lotteries Act 2011. 18+ for the rest of this site.
